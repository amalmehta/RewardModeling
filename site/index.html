What is invented here

Everything on this page is computed from synthetic data. These are invented parameters, chosen by us rather than measured: the 8×8 layout, slip probability 0.1, γ = 0.95, horizon 20, the goal, lava and multi-goal values, the rescaling of every reward to unit spread, the labeler's rationality β = 1, the ε-greedy mixture that generates compared trajectories, and the demonstrator's temperature. The labelers are simulated, not people. Results describe this setting, not reward learning in general.
